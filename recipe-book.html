Nigerian Jollof Rice

Recipe Details

  • Preparation Time: 45 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 60 minutes
  • Servings: 8
  • Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Basmati rice 6 cups
Fresh tomatoes 3 cups
Peppers 1 and 1/2 cups
Onions 2 large bulbs
Tomato paste 1/2 cup
Vegetable oil 1/2 cup
Seasoning cubes 5 cubes
Jollof spices: Spicity, Benny, Rosemary leaf, Curry, Thyme 1 and 1/2 tbsp each
Salt To taste
Chicken 1 kilo
Chicken stock 2 cups

Steps

  1. Parboil the rice and set aside.
  2. Wash the chicken, marinate and fry.
  3. Blend tomatoes, pepper and onions.
  4. Heat oil and fry the blended mixture.
  5. Add seasoning, jollof spices and chicken stock.
  6. Pour in the parboiled rice.
  7. Cook until rice is soft and liquid is absorbed.
  8. Garnish with veggies and stir
  9. Serve with chicken.

Fluffy Pancakes

Recipe Details

  • Preparation Time: 20 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 10 minutes
  • Servings: 4
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Flour 2 cups
Milk 1½ cups
Eggs 2
Sugar 2 tbsp
Baking powder 2 tsp

Steps

  1. Mix all dry ingredients.
  2. Add eggs and milk.
  3. Whisk until smooth.
  4. Pour batter onto a hot pan.
  5. Cook both sides until golden brown.
  6. Serve with syrup or fruits.

Recipe Source: Pancake Recipes -Wikipedia

Fresh Fruit Salad

Recipe Details

  • Preparation Time: 30 minutes
  • Cooking Time: None
  • Servings: 5
  • Difficulty: Very Easy

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Apples 2
Bananas 2
Grapes 1 cup
Watermelon 1 large
cucumber 2 big one
Golden melon 1 large
Pineapple 1 large

Steps

  • Wash all fruits thoroughly.
  • Cut into bite-sized pieces.
  • Mix together in a large bowl.
  • Chill before serving.
  • Serve with either greek yoghurt, condensed milk or whipped cream.

Recipe Source: Fruit Salad Recipes -Wikipedia
