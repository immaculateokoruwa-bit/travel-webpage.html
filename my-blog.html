My Learning Journey

Starting my web development journey has been both exciting and challenging. Every lesson introduces a new concept, from writing simple HTML pages to styling them with CSS. Although learning a new skill requires patience, I enjoy seeing my ideas come to life in a web browser. I have also learned the importance of practice and consistency. Completing assignments has helped me improve my understanding of web design, and I am becoming more confident in creating responsive and visually appealing webpages.
