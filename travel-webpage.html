Why Visit Calabar?

Calabar, the capital of Cross River State, is one of Nigeria's most beautiful and peaceful cities. Popularly known as the Tourism Capital of Nigeria, Calabar is famous for its rich cultural heritage, friendly people, beautiful scenery, and exciting festivals.

Whether you are looking for relaxation, adventure, history, or entertainment, Calabar has something for everyone. From breathtaking resorts to wildlife parks and colourful cultural events, every visit promises unforgettable memories.

Things to Do

  1. Attend the famous Calabar Carnival.
  2. Take a boat cruise at Marina Resort.
  3. Visit the National Museum and learn about Calabar's history.
  4. Explore the rainforest at Cross River National Park.
  5. Enjoy the cool weather at Obudu Mountain Resort.
  6. Taste delicious local dishes like Edikang Ikong and Afang Soup.

Calabar is the perfect destination for families, couples, adventure seekers, and culture lovers. Its clean environment, warm hospitality, delicious cuisine, and world-class attractions make it one of the best places to visit in Nigeria.
